## Competitors per ICP

### ICP-A: Legal PY

| Competitor | What they sell | Their weakness | Our counter-positioning |
|------------|----------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **Hostinger** (re: trademark) | Generic web hosting | Doesn't know legal workflows | We build legal-specific (intake forms, contract automation, compliance calendar) |
| **Local web agencies (PY)** | Generic web + basic CRM | No AI capability | We add AI summarization of case files, client Q&A bot, document review |
| **International legal-tech (e.g., Clio)** | Practice management | Expensive ($49-$99/user/mo), English-only | We charge per-firm flat, Spanish/Guaraní support, lower price |

### ICP-B: Coaching PY + EU

| Competitor | What they sell | Their weakness | Our counter-positioning |
|------------|----------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **BetterUp** | Enterprise coaching platform | $200-$500/user/mo, US-only | We target solopreneurs/coaches, $30-$100/mo |
| **CoachHub** | Enterprise coaching platform | Same: enterprise-only, $200+/user | Same: SMB-friendly, multilingual |
| **Marketplace coaches** (1-on-1 platforms) | Direct coach matching | $100+ per session, no AI | We offer AI-augmented coaching for 1/10th the cost |

### ICP-C: Mid-market NL/EU AI-curious

| Competitor | What they sell | Their weakness | Our counter-positioning |
|------------|----------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **NL AI consultancies (e.g., AI.nl)** | Strategy + implementation | €10K+ per project, slow | We ship in 2-4 weeks, fixed scope |
| **Big-4 AI practices** (Accenture, Deloitte) | Enterprise AI | Six-figure projects | SMB-friendly pricing, scope |
| **Internal hiring** | Build in-house | 6-12mo ramp, $100K+ salaries | We augment, not replace |
