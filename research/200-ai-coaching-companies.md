# 197 AI Coaching Companies — What They All Do

> Research compiled 2026-08-14 by Erebus for AI Whisperers Paraguay EAS — vertical-research expansion triggered by `mark-nl-vastgoed-coaching` (NL real estate coaching, active prospect, Aug 2026).
> 197 companies across 18 categories. Sources: 56 ddgs queries (791 hits, 502 unique URLs) + canonical knowledge of public AI ecosystem + the 56-repo Ai-Whisperers inventory.
> **Not an exhaustive list** — these are the most relevant by category, weighted toward (a) production-stage companies with real revenue or large raises, (b) verticals that map to AI Whisperers' actual capabilities, and (c) categories already represented in our org (sales enablement, leadership, sales-call coaching, mental wellness, learning).

## TL;DR — the landscape in one paragraph

The AI-coaching-for-companies market is **the fastest-growing segment of corporate L&D**, valued at **USD 5.48B in 2025 → USD 6.69B in 2026 (CAGR ~22%)** with credible forecasts to **USD 15B+ by 2028-2030** (28.5% CAGR — Source: Research and Markets, Gitnux 2026 stats report, Careertrainer.ai). It's split into 7 architectural buckets: **(1) executive / human-coaching platforms** (BetterUp $4.7B valuation, CoachHub $373M raised — hybrid AI+human), **(2) AI-only conversational coaches** (Valence $50M Series B, Delphi $16M Series A, Yoodli $40M Series B, Second Nature $38M, Ezra, Poised, Tomo), **(3) sales-call coaching** (Gong $500M ARR, Chorus/ZoomInfo, Mindtickle $1.2B unicorn, Salesloft $245M raised, Hyperbound $15M Series A, Othello $6.85M seed), **(4) conversation intelligence / sales enablement** (Outreach, Salesloft, Allego, Showpad, Brainshark), **(5) corporate L&D / skill coaching** (Valence, AceUp $22.5M, Numly, Risely, Multiverse, Slingshot AI $30M), **(6) mental wellness + AI** (Woebot, Wysa, Slingshot AI, Spring Health, Lyra, Modern Health, Headspace, Calm), and **(7) adjacent enablers** (CRM, LMS, contact center, roleplay). The most-funded vertical is **conversational AI for sales teams** (Gong, Outreach, Salesloft, Mindtickle — combined > $1B raised). The **most defensible moat** is **human-coach network + AI matching** (BetterUp, CoachHub), because the LLM is commoditizing and the coach supply is the lock-in. The **trillion-dollar question** for AI Whisperers: can we ship a **trilingual (es/en/nl) coaching agent** for the **mid-market** that the giants don't serve?

**Total**: 197 companies across 18 categories. The org has zero direct competitors in Paraguay, zero in Dutch-market coaching, and one active prospect (Mark NL) that already validates the model.

## Cross-cutting notes on this landscape

### Funding pattern by tier (2024-2026)

| Tier | Capital deployed | Trend |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| **Conversation intelligence + sales-call coaching** | $1B+ raised (Gong, Outreach, Salesloft, Mindtickle, Chorus) | Mature; consolidation |
| **AI-native enterprise coaching (Valence, AceUp)** | $200M+ | Hot; biggest growth 2025-2026 |
| **AI communication coaching (Yoodli, Poised)** | $50M+ | Growing fast; mid-market accessible |
| **Mental wellness AI (Woebot, Wysa, Slingshot)** | $300M+ | Converging with coaching |
| **Career coaching AI (Blomma, Tomo)** | $10-50M | Consumer segment |

### Three structural patterns

1. **The human-coach network is the moat** for BetterUp, CoachHub, Sounding Board. They spend 5+ years building coach supply; AIW cannot replicate this without partnerships.
2. **The AI-only coach is the structurally faster-growing model** (Valence, Yoodli). Better unit economics; scales infinitely.
3. **The mid-market is underserved**. Big-4 won't touch <$100K ACV deals; BetterUp/CoachHub focus on enterprise ($50K+ ACV). A trilingual mid-market AI coach at $5-15K ACV + $500-2K/mo MRR is a clear gap.

### Regulation that affects this market (2025-2026)

- **EU AI Act** (enforced 2026) — Article 50 transparency; coaching AI for employees may fall under "high-risk" if used for performance evaluation. **An EU AI Act-compliant coaching product is a real moat for AIW** because US companies won't bother.
- **GDPR** — call recording + voice biometric data is regulated; AI coaches that transcribe calls need DPIA + opt-in flow.
- **ICF (International Coaching Federation)** — the credentialing body for human coaches; most AI coaching companies position "AI-augmented ICF coaches."
- **HIPAA** (US) — mental-health AI requires BAAs. EU equivalent: GDPR Art 9 special-category data.
