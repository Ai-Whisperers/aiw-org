# 1000 Questions to Understand a Company — and AI Whisperers' Answers

> Compiled 2026-08-13 by Erebus. **The 1000-question discovery framework** used by VCs, strategy consultants, and growth-stage due diligence teams — synthesized from canonical sources (Sean Ellis PMF survey, Business Model Canvas, Lean Canvas, 5C Analysis, Porter's Five Forces, BCG Matrix, SWOT, Steve Blank Customer Development, B2B Discovery Playbooks) and applied to **AI Whisperers Paraguay EAS** with real, sourced answers from the org.

> **Use this doc 2 ways**:
> 1. As a **template** — when analyzing any new client/company, work through these questions in order
> 2. As a **self-assessment** — for AI Whisperers itself, the answers below show what's known, what's guessed, and what's missing

## Category 1: Identity & Mission (35 questions)

### Founding Story
