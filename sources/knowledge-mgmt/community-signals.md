# Knowledge Management — Community Signals

> Maintained by Echo community scanner

## Monitored communities

| Platform | Target | Scan cadence |
|----------|--------|--------------|
| Reddit | r/KnowledgeManagement, r/semanticweb, r/MachineLearning (document AI threads) | weekly |
| Hacker News | document intelligence, ontology, provenance, metadata threads | weekly |
| W3C | dublin-core, prov-wg mailing lists | monthly |
| LinkedIn | knowledge management practitioners (manual sample) | monthly |

## Signal types to extract

- Document classification and metadata best practices
- Ontology and taxonomy tooling recommendations
- Provenance and lineage patterns (PROV-O, W3C)
- Anti-patterns in knowledge base maintenance
- Emerging standards for document intelligence pipelines

## Output

Writes to `sources/knowledge-mgmt/community-signals.md` (append by date).
