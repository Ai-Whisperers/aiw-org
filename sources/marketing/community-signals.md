# Marketing — Community Signals

> Maintained by Echo community scanner

## Monitored communities

| Platform | Target | Scan cadence |
|----------|--------|--------------|
| Reddit | r/marketing, r/startups, r/Entrepreneur | weekly |
| Hacker News | marketing, growth, SaaS threads | weekly |
| Indie Hackers | growth stories | weekly |
| LinkedIn | practitioner posts (manual sample) | monthly |

- New channel tactics with evidence
- Tool recommendations (OSS preferred)
- Anti-patterns / failures
- ICP language shifts

## Output

Writes to `sources/marketing/community-signals.md` (append by date).
