## Usage in conversation

When Ivan says **"@Saleina is stalled on 2 leads today"** — everyone knows he means `sales-pipeline`. The name hints at the function (sales) and is single-word, log-friendly.

When Kiki says **"@Devin flagged 3 hard-stop bypasses"** — but wait, the AI safety agent is actually **@Safina**. That's intentional: **@Devin** is the chief (engineering-roster), **@Safina** is the AI-safety watcher (ai-safety-engineer). The naming pattern lets two related agents have distinct single-word names.

When the coaching system needs to escalate: **@Coachina** (customers) hands off to **@Coachin** (conversion) hands off to **@Onboardia** (onboarding). The -ina / -in / -ia variants within the coaching family suggest role hierarchy.
