## The org in one paragraph

Ai-Whisperers is a **Paraguay-based AI-native consulting firm** building agentic operations for small and medium businesses. We deploy production AI agents — not demos, not chatbots — that run full business functions: finance reporting, sales qualification, engineering deploy monitoring, customer coaching, and research tracking. Our flagship agent-org framework turns a 2-person founding team into a 7-agent organization within 30 days.

## The problem

Small and medium businesses in Latin America — and globally — face an operations gap that traditional consulting doesn't solve. They can't afford a $300K/year McKinsey engagement, but they also can't operate without some form of finance, sales, engineering, research, and customer ops function. Most SMBs run on a single founder's attention, which doesn't scale and burns out the founder.

## The solution

We deploy a **complete agentic operations team** in days, not months:
- **Finance controller agent** — daily cash-flow + runway brief, deal pipeline reporting
- **Sales pipeline agent** — inbound qualification, lead enrichment, proposal drafting
- **Engineering roster agent** — deploy health, PR tracking, infra cost monitoring
- **Research tracker agent** — market research, thesis work, course pipeline
- **Customer coaching agent** — onboarding, education, retention
- **Business analyst agent** — daily brief for the founder
- **Management coordinator agent** — weekly cross-repo review

The agents run on open-source infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers, n8n, LiteLLM, Evolution API) so the marginal cost is near-zero. The founder stays in the loop on decisions, but 90% of mechanical work is automated.
