# Tax Structure Comparison — PY / NL / EU / Hybrid

> **Phase 8 Area #18** | Finance & Legal dept | Owner: Ivan + external accountant + tax advisor
> **Date**: 2026-09-01
> **Status**: Research complete; decision pending

---

## The 3 candidate structures

### Option A: PY EAS only (current)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Tax rate** | IRP (5%-10% for SMEs) + IVA (10%) |
| **Compliance burden** | Low |
| **Cost** | Annual filing fees ~$500 |
| **Growth ceiling** | Limited for EU/US clients (no local entity) |
| **Currency exposure** | PYG/USD only |
| **Best for** | PY-only clients, low volume |

### Option B: PY EAS + Dutch BV (besloten vennootschap)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Tax rate** | NL corporate 19% (€200K threshold); 25.8% above |
| **Compliance burden** | Medium (Dutch accounting + annual filing) |
| **Cost** | €1K-€3K/year for accountant + €300 BV registration |
| **Growth ceiling** | High for EU clients (local invoicing) |
| **Currency exposure** | EUR/USD/PYG |
| **Best for** | EU-clients primary, PY secondary |

### Option C: PY EAS + Estonian e-Residency + invoicing platform

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Tax rate** | 0% corporate on retained earnings (20% on distribution) |
| **Compliance burden** | Medium (e-Residency + quarterly filing) |
| **Cost** | €100 e-Residency + €500/year platform |
| **Growth ceiling** | High for global (digital-only) |
| **Currency exposure** | Any |
| **Best for** | Digital-first, low presence |

## Recommendation: **Stay with A (PY EAS only) for now**, plan B in 12 months

### Reasoning

1. **Current MRR $240** — no tax structure matters until revenue grows
2. **EU AI Act compliance is the bigger risk** than tax structure right now
3. **PY EAS already registered** — switching adds complexity, not value
4. **When MRR > $5K or 5+ EU clients**: re-evaluate Option B

### When to revisit

- MRR > $5K
- 5+ EU clients (establishes NL presence)
- >€10K PY-NL transfer needs (regulatory triggers)
- 12 months from now (2026-09 or 2027-09)

---

## Caveats

- This is **research**, not legal/tax advice. Engage an actual accountant before changing structure.
- NL BV has implications for VAT (BTW), payroll, etc.
- Estonian e-Residency requires physical appearance once for card pickup
